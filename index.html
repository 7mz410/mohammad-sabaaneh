MOHAMMAD SABAANEH

Cartoonist & Academic

ABOUT

Mohammad Sabaaneh is a Palestinian cartoonist and academic whose prominent career in art began in 2002. He teaches at the Arab American University and holds a Master’s degree in Illustration from the United Kingdom, which he earned through the prestigious Chevening Scholarship.

His work has been published in numerous newspapers and magazines, including The Washington Post, Al-Quds Al-Arabi, and Al-Hayat. He is a member of Cartoon Movement and serves as the Middle East representative for Cartoonists Rights (formerly CRNI).

Sabaaneh has received several awards and honors, including the Arab Cartoon Competition Award in 2013, first prize at the Estack International Cartoon Festival in 2017, and the EWK Award in 2024. Among his most recent accolades is the Broken Frontier Award in 2025, in recognition of his outstanding contributions to the field of comics.

In addition to his artistry, Sabaaneh operates as a cultural curator, having managed several major international exhibitions in Palestine, and his books such as "White and Black", "Power Born of Dreams", "30 Seconds from Gaza", and "Welcome to Hell" have received global recognition.
